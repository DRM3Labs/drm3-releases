# Privacy Policy — DRM3 Labs Corp.

**Last updated: May 14, 2026**

## What We Collect

DRM3 products are designed for privacy. We collect minimal data:

- **Pistachio (inference client):** Runs locally on your machine. We do not collect prompts, responses, or personal data. Pistachio transmits your wallet address (publicly visible on-chain), install identity (Ed25519 public key), and software version to DRM3 infrastructure for network monitoring and pass capacity verification. See [Pistachio Privacy](pistachio/PRIVACY.md) for details.
- **DRM3 Explorer:** Reads publicly available blockchain data. API requests include wallet address and install identity headers for rate limiting and capacity verification.
- **NFT marketplace:** Wallet addresses are visible on-chain by nature of blockchain transactions. We do not collect email, name, or personal information.

## What We Don't Collect

- No email addresses (unless you contact us directly)
- No personal identification
- No inference prompts or responses
- No private keys or wallet credentials
- No browsing history or cookies
- No analytics or tracking scripts on any DRM3 site
- No IP address logging

## Private Key Handling

DRM3 products that require a private key store it **exclusively on your local device**. Your private key is never transmitted to DRM3 servers or any third party. DRM3 Labs Corp. has no access to your private key at any time.

## Blockchain Data

Wallet addresses and transaction data are public on Base mainnet. This is inherent to blockchain technology, not a choice we make.

## Third-Party Services

Pistachio connects to third-party RPC providers (e.g., Alchemy) configured by the user, and to AI providers on the Morpheus network. These third parties have their own privacy policies. DRM3 Labs Corp. does not control their data practices.

## Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit user data to third parties for advertising, marketing, or any commercial purpose.
